**1. Rising Demand for Sustainable Packaging Solutions**

Containerboard, which is typically made from recycled materials, offers a sustainable alternative to plastic packaging. The Thai government has also introduced policies and regulations promoting sustainability in packaging, encouraging businesses to adopt more environmentally responsible materials. This regulatory push, combined with consumer demand for eco-friendly options, is expected to continue driving the growth of the containerboard market in the coming years.

The rapid growth of e-commerce and online retail has had a profound impact on the demand for packaging materials, particularly containerboard. With more consumers shifting to online shopping, the need for efficient, reliable, and sustainable packaging solutions has surged. Containerboard is widely used for shipping products in cardboard boxes, which are essential for protecting goods during transportation.

Thailand, being a hub for e-commerce in Southeast Asia, has witnessed significant growth in online retail, further fueling the demand for containerboard. As e-commerce platforms expand and more businesses move online, the containerboard market is expected to see continued growth as companies seek robust and cost-effective packaging solutions for their products.

The food and beverage sector in Thailand is experiencing rapid growth, which is directly contributing to the demand for containerboard.
